ADMINISTRATOR · LOYALTY

Rewards management

Earning rules

Whole spend blocks only. For example, 1 point per $100 gives 2 points for $250 of items. Rules are saved with new orders. Points are awarded after payment is marked paid and the handover PIN completes delivery or collection. Earlier orders are not backfilled.

Reward catalogue

Rewards are collected from the store; they do not automatically discount a shopping basket. Hiding a reward preserves existing claims and their original terms.

Customer balances

Reward collection queue

Match the customer's name and claim reference before handing over a reward. Cancelling a pending claim returns its points once.

Downgrade customer account owner to employee

Their login and memberships in other businesses stay with this account. Their former business package, documents, discussions, members and staff assignments are retained but unavailable while this account is an employee. Upgrading it again restores that business access.

The account will be signed out and must sign in again as an employee. Their private administrator chat remains available.

Upgrade customer to staff

Staff can use team chat and manage only assigned customers, orders and parcels. This does not grant administrator access. Customer records are retained, but the account will use the staff portal. The person must sign in again.

Upgrade employee to customer account owner

This adds their own business portal. Their login, existing memberships and administrator conversation are preserved.

Business employees and members

Add an existing employee login to this business. Access is active immediately; their password and recovery code stay unchanged.

Assigned staff

Assigned staff can manage this business's package and customer discussion. Administrators retain access to all accounts.

Recover selected account

Verify the customer's identity before resetting their account. Staff accounts require an administrator. You cannot reset your own account here.

Share these credentials privately with the verified owner. They are shown only for this reset. The previous recovery code is invalid.

The customer must choose a new password. They will receive a replacement recovery code after that change.

Delete selected customer account

This permanently deletes the customer's login, package details, messages, and saved ledger entries. It cannot be undone through the portal. Linked documents themselves are not deleted. If this account owns a shared business, its membership links are removed too. Members keep their separate logins and their own businesses.
