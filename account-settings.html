Push notifications

Receive a reminder on this device when someone sends a message in a conversation available to your account, even when the portal page is closed.

OOC (Out of character) notice

These notifications are an out-of-character convenience. Receiving an alert does not give your character in-game knowledge. Keep OOC reminders separate from what your character learns through roleplay.

Lock-screen alerts show a generic reminder without names or message contents. Open the portal and sign in to read the message.

Enable notifications separately on each device. Alerts can continue after signing out; choose “Disable on this device” before signing out on a shared device. Enabling push for another account in the same browser switches this device to that account.

Quick guide: install Umai Mart as an app

Use your real phone or computer browser and open the delivery portal. Installation and push alerts are not supported in the in-game phone.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the portal in Safari and tap Share.
  2. Select Add to Home Screen. If shown, keep Open as Web App switched on, then tap Add.
  3. Open the new Umai Mart icon from your Home Screen and sign in.
  4. Open Account settings, select Enable push notifications and allow notifications. Home Screen push requires iOS/iPadOS 16.4 or later.

Android

  1. Open the portal in Chrome and tap the three-dot menu.
  2. Choose Install and create shortcut → Install, Install app or Add to Home screen, depending on your browser version.
  3. Open the installed app, sign in, then enable push in Account settings and allow notifications.

Desktop

  1. Open the portal in Chrome or Edge and look for the Install icon in the address bar. In Chrome you can also use Menu → Cast, save, and share → Install page as app.
  2. Follow the installation prompt, then enable push in Account settings. Supported desktop browsers can also receive push without installing the app.

If alerts do not arrive, check browser and device notification permissions and Focus/Do Not Disturb settings. Delivery is not guaranteed. The portal bell remains available.

Your account settings

Your account can stay signed in on two browser sessions at once. A third sign-in replaces the oldest. Password changes and account recovery sign out both sessions.

These settings apply to your own account, not a business you joined. Changing your username signs you out and replaces your recovery code. Your package and messages stay with your account.
