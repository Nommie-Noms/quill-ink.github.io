Administrator push notifications

Receive alerts on this device for new client messages and employee messages to administrators, even when the portal page is closed.

On iPhone or iPad, add the portal to your Home Screen and open it there before enabling notifications. Delivery depends on browser, device and notification settings. The in-game phone may not support push.

Notifications use a generic preview. Open the portal and sign in to read messages.

Your account settings

These settings apply to your own account, not a business you joined. Changing your username signs you out and replaces your recovery code. Your package and messages stay with your account.

Change your password

This changes only your own login. Other business members keep their passwords.

Members of your business

You own this business. Invite an existing customer username; they must accept in their Account Settings. Members share your package, documents and discussion, but cannot change your business name or login.
